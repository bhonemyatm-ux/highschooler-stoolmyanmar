About the College

Co-operative College (Mandalay) is located in Patheingyi Township, Mandalay, around the Yan Kin Hill area (as listed in public directory information). The Ministry of Cooperatives and Rural Development (MCRD) states that Co-operative Colleges (including Mandalay) run 2-year diploma training and, after completion, students can bridge to co-operative & management universities.
