About UCMT (Official)

University of Co-operative and Management, Thanlyin is located in Nyaung Thone Bin Village, Thanlyin, Yangon (South District), about 8 miles from the south-eastern part of Yangon, near Thanlyin–Kyaik Khauk Pagoda Road, and close to the Yangon–Thilawa Railway and East Yangon University. The total area of the university is 208.209 acres.

Vision & Missions

Vision: To be a supportive university for community development by nurturing human resources who are qualified and educated social business persons with national and social spirits.

Missions:

  • To train and bring up human resources needed to increase socio-economic development.
  • To train youths to be good citizens who are knowledgeable and rich in critical thinking.
  • To consistently conduct research programmes.

Background History (Timeline)

These milestones are stated in UCMT’s official background history.

🏫

1970

Co-operative Training School established in Mayangone Township, Chawtwingone, Yangon; later moved to Thanlyin.

📢

21 June 1994

Upgraded to Yangon Regional College under Notification (10/94) (dated 19-5-1994).

🎓

11 Nov 1996

Upgraded and renamed as Yangon Co-operative Degree College under Notification (17/96).

🏛️

2012 (21 June)

Upgraded to Co-operative University, Thanlyin under Notification (29/2012) following Government meeting (20/2012).

📝

2021 (Nov)

Renamed to University of Co-operative and Management, Thanlyin under the stated ministry notifications (MCRD and MoE).

Degrees & Postgraduate (Official)

UCMT’s official “Degrees awarded” explains how degrees changed over time.

🎓

B.A (Business Science) → B.B.Sc

B.A (Business Science) was conferred up to 2009–2010, and B.B.Sc started from 2010–2011 (official).

📚

Specialized B.B.Sc (since 2014–2015)

From 2014–2015, undergraduate degrees are taught by specialization: Social Enterprise Management, Regional Development, Accounting & Finance, Marketing Management, and Applied Statistics (official).

🧾

Postgraduate Diploma (1 year)

Postgraduate diploma programs by specialization are listed as 1-year courses (official degrees page).

🏅

Master’s courses

Master’s programs by specialization are listed on the official degrees page (started from 2015–2016 on that page).
