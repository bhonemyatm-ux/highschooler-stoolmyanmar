01

Scholarship Overview

The Fellowship Scholarship is Ho Chi Minh City Open University's premier doctoral scholarship programme designed to attract talented researchers to pursue PhD degrees at HCMC OU while contributing to the university's teaching and research mission. This comprehensive scholarship provides 100% tuition fee waiver for the entire duration of doctoral studies PLUS monthly living expenses support of VND 10,000,000 (approximately USD 400) - making it a fully-funded PhD opportunity in Southeast Asia.

In exchange for this generous support, Fellowship Scholars serve as Teaching Assistants (leading 8 classes per year) or Research Assistants (conducting research in English). This model benefits both scholars and the university: scholars gain valuable teaching/research experience while earning their doctorate fully funded, and HCMC OU develops its academic workforce and research capacity. PhD programmes at HCMC OU typically take 3-4 years to complete, covering diverse fields from Business Administration to Information Technology, Social Sciences, and more. The university has granted over 1,000+ doctoral degrees and is expanding its research capabilities.

✨

Why Fellowship Scholarship is Excellent for Myanmar PhD Candidates

This is RARE fully-funded PhD opportunity in Southeast Asia! 100% tuition PLUS living stipend (VND 10 million/month = ~$400 USD) means you can focus ENTIRELY on research without financial stress! Work as TA/RA gives valuable academic experience - teaching or research while studying (excellent for future academic career!). Study in Vietnam - close to Myanmar (1-2 hour flight, easy family visits), similar culture, affordable living (stipend covers basic needs!), safe environment. HCMC is ASEAN's startup & business hub - excellent for applied research, data collection, industry partnerships. After PhD, strong position for academic jobs in Myanmar universities OR regional/international positions. Rolling admissions = apply anytime! Myanmar scholars increasingly choosing Vietnam for PhD - growing community, supportive environment.

03

How to Apply

1

Identify Research Area & Potential Supervisor

Research HCMC OU faculty in your field of interest. Visit university website, read faculty publications, identify professors whose research aligns with your interests. Contact potential supervisors via email introducing yourself, sharing research interests, asking if they're accepting PhD students. Building supervisor relationship early is CRITICAL!

2

Develop Strong Research Proposal

Write comprehensive PhD research proposal (10-15 pages): (1) Research problem/questions, (2) Literature review showing gap, (3) Theoretical framework, (4) Methodology, (5) Expected contributions, (6) Timeline. Make it specific, feasible, innovative! Proposal quality is PRIMARY factor in PhD admission. Get feedback from potential supervisor!

3

Gather Academic Documents

Collect: Bachelor's + Master's transcripts and certificates, academic CV (highlight research experience, publications if any), 2-3 strong recommendation letters from professors/supervisors who know your research capability, English test scores (IELTS/TOEFL), passport copy. Get notarized English translations if documents in other languages.

4

Submit PhD Programme Application

Contact HCMC OU Graduate School (email or visit website) for PhD application procedures. Complete PhD programme application form. Submit all documents including research proposal. Pay application fee if required. Work with identified supervisor on strengthening application.

5

Apply for Fellowship Scholarship

Once PhD admission is likely/confirmed, submit Fellowship Scholarship application separately. Express interest in Teaching Assistant OR Research Assistant position. Explain how your skills/experience qualify you for TA/RA work. Highlight teaching experience (if applying for TA) or research projects (if applying for RA).

6

Prepare for Scholarship Interview

If shortlisted for Fellowship, prepare thoroughly for interview with Scholarship Council! Practice presenting research proposal clearly, explaining teaching/research capabilities, demonstrating English communication skills. Be ready to discuss: why HCMC OU, how you'll contribute as TA/RA, career goals after PhD. Interview may be in-person or virtual (Zoom).

7

Accept Offer & Arrange Visa

If awarded Fellowship Scholarship - congratulations! Accept PhD admission and scholarship offer. Sign contract specifying TA/RA duties. Apply for Vietnam student visa using admission letter and scholarship confirmation. University provides visa support letter. Visa processing typically 1-2 weeks.

8

Relocate to HCMC & Begin PhD

Fly to Ho Chi Minh City, find accommodation (budget $100-200/month for basic apartment), register with university, attend PhD orientation, begin coursework and TA/RA duties. Connect with PhD student community, settle into research life. Your journey to doctorate begins!

📌 Critical Application Tips:

  • Supervisor Connection First: Don't apply "blind" - establish relationship with potential supervisor before formal application
  • Research Proposal Quality: This is most important factor - invest significant time!
  • Publications Help: If you have research publications (even from master's thesis), highlight them!
  • Teaching/Research Experience: Any prior TA, RA, or teaching experience strengthens Fellowship application
  • Interview Preparation: Practice extensively - interview determines Fellowship award!
04

Pro Tips for Success

🎓

Research Proposal Makes or Breaks Application

Your research proposal is EVERYTHING for PhD admission and Fellowship! Spend weeks developing it: deep literature review, clear research gap, innovative approach, feasible methodology. Vietnam context is plus: "Comparative study of Myanmar-Vietnam SME financing" better than purely Myanmar-focused (harder data access). Show you understand BOTH theory and practical application. Get multiple feedback rounds from advisor/professors before submission!

💰

VND 10M Stipend Is Liveable in HCMC

VND 10,000,000/month (~$400 USD) may sound small but it's liveable for focused PhD student in HCMC! Budget breakdown: Accommodation $150 (shared apartment near university), Food $150 (cook at home, occasional street food $2/meal), Transport $30 (bike or bus), Utilities $30, Remaining $40 for personal. Live modestly, focus on research. Many PhD students supplement with freelance work (editing, tutoring $10-20/hr) but TA/RA keeps you busy!

👨‍🏫

TA Experience Is Career Gold

Teaching 8 classes/year as TA is AMAZING for academic career! Universities worldwide value teaching experience when hiring faculty. Keep teaching portfolio: syllabi, lecture slides, student evaluations. Document pedagogical approaches. If you plan academic career in Myanmar or internationally, this TA experience makes you highly competitive! Plus, teaching deepens your own understanding - best way to learn is teach!

🔬

Publication Strategy During PhD

Most PhD programmes require publications for graduation. Start early! Year 1: literature review paper in regional journal. Year 2-3: empirical findings in international journals. Year 4: dissertation completion. Vietnam journals easier to publish initially (Vietnamese Journal of Economics, Asian Business Review). Build up to international ISI/Scopus journals. 2-3 publications typical for PhD graduation at HCMC OU. Work closely with supervisor on publication strategy!

🌏

Leverage HCMC Research Context

HCMC offers unique research advantages: large diverse population for surveys, many companies for business research access, tech startups for innovation studies, expat community for cross-cultural research. Use HCMC as "research laboratory" while studying Myanmar-Vietnam comparisons or regional ASEAN topics. Example: "Digital payment adoption in Vietnam and Myanmar" - collect Vietnam data easily while in HCMC, Myanmar data during visits home!

🤝

Build Myanmar PhD Network

Growing community of Myanmar PhD students across Vietnam universities! Connect via Facebook groups, organize meetups, share experiences. Support network crucial for isolation periods during PhD. Myanmar students help each other: research advice, emotional support, Myanmar food cooking together, celebrating Thingyan! Also connect with Vietnamese PhD students - cultural exchange enriches experience. Don't isolate yourself!

📚

PhD is Marathon, Not Sprint

3-4 years is LONG time - pace yourself! Year 1 toughest (coursework, adjustment, language challenges). Year 2-3 research phase (data collection, analysis - most productive). Year 4 writing phase (dissertation assembly, revisions, defense prep). Expect setbacks: data collection issues, research pivots, publication rejections. Normal! Maintain work-life balance: exercise, hobbies, friendships. Burned-out researcher produces poor research. Sustainable pace yields quality PhD!

🎯

Post-PhD Career Planning

Start career planning from Year 1! Options: (1) Academia - apply for lecturer positions at Myanmar universities (desperately need PhDs!), (2) International academia - continue with postdoc abroad, (3) Research institutions - think tanks, NGOs, (4) Industry - companies increasingly hire PhDs for R&D, analytics. Build CV during PhD: publications, conference presentations, grants, teaching portfolio. Network at conferences. LinkedIn presence. PhD + teaching experience = excellent Myanmar university lecturer candidate!
