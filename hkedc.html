About (GTI / TVET)

Government Technical Institutes (GTIs) provide technical and vocational education focused on hands-on learning and industry-ready skills. GTI programs typically run in a structured multi-year diploma pathway.

GTI (Putao) has official contact information published by DTVET, and students should confirm the latest intake/major availability with the institute.

Diploma Programs (Common GTI Majors)

The GTI Putao site lists these courses as the standard GTI diploma areas, and notes that not every school offers every course.

🏗️

Civil Engineering

A.G.T.I Diploma

Construction fundamentals, drawing, and basic civil engineering practice.

⚡

Electrical Power Engineering

A.G.T.I Diploma

Electrical power basics, installation practice, and lab skills.

📡

Electronic Engineering

A.G.T.I Diploma

Electronics fundamentals, circuits, and practical troubleshooting.

⚙️

Mechanical Engineering

A.G.T.I Diploma

Workshop practice, machines, and maintenance fundamentals.

💻

Information Technology Engineering

A.G.T.I Diploma

Core IT skills such as programming basics and networking foundations.

Note: The GTI Putao site states that all courses are not available in every school, and some schools open only 2–3 courses based on local needs. Please confirm the current open majors with GTI (Putao).
