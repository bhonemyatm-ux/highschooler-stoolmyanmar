About the University

Meiktila University of Economics is located in Meiktila, Mandalay Region, and is one of Myanmar’s universities focused on economics and business. It offers undergraduate and graduate degrees and diplomas, mostly in commerce, statistics, and economics, and it also has graduate programs such as MBA, MPA and PhD programs.
