Scholarship Overview

The Japanese Government (MEXT) Scholarship is one of the world's most prestigious and comprehensive scholarship programs, providing approximately 9,000 scholarships annually to international students. This fully-funded program supports talented Myanmar students pursuing undergraduate, Master's, and PhD studies at top Japanese universities with complete financial coverage.

MEXT scholarships provide full tuition waiver, generous monthly living stipends ranging from ¥117,000 to ¥145,000 (approximately $780-$970 USD), round-trip airfare between Myanmar and Japan, and comprehensive Japanese language training for 6-12 months before degree programs begin. For Myanmar students, applications are processed through the Myanmar Association of Japan Alumni (MAJA) in coordination with the Embassy of Japan in Yangon.

✨

What Makes This Special?

MEXT is funded directly by Japan's Ministry of Education, Culture, Sports, Science & Technology (Monbukagakusho). It offers the longest scholarship duration (up to 7 years for graduate students including preparatory period), includes mandatory Japanese language training, provides guaranteed university placement, and requires NO prior Japanese language knowledge. Scholars join a prestigious global alumni network spanning over 60 years across 160+ countries.

How to Apply

1

Prepare Your Documents (Start 2-3 Months Early)

Gather all required documents including official transcripts, graduation certificates, recommendation letters, research plan (for graduate students), and CV. Ensure all documents are properly certified by your university/institution and translated to English if necessary.

Download Application Forms →
2

Online Registration via MAJA

Between April 14-May 6, 2026, complete online registration through the Google Form provided by Myanmar Association of Japan Alumni (MAJA). You'll receive a confirmation email with your registration number and further instructions.

Visit MAJA Facebook Page →
3

Submit Complete Application Package

Submit your complete application package to MAJA office at Thingangyun Township, Yangon by the deadline (May 14 for Research, May 26 for Undergraduate) at 3:00 PM sharp. Can be submitted in person or by registered post. Keep copies of all documents!

4

Attend Written Examination

Eligible candidates will be notified via email. Attend the written test at designated exam station in Yangon. Test covers Japanese/English language proficiency and subject-specific topics related to your field of study.

5

Interview at Embassy of Japan

Qualified candidates from the written test will be invited for an interview at the Embassy of Japan in Yangon. Be prepared to discuss your study plan, research motivation, future goals, and why Japan in clear English.

6

University Placement Process

If selected as a scholarship recipient, MEXT will coordinate with Japanese universities based on your field preferences and research plan. You may need to contact professors and request Letters of Acceptance from universities.

7

Final Notification & Preparation

Successful candidates receive official notification via email around June 2026. Begin visa application process, attend pre-departure orientation, and prepare for departure to Japan in April 2027.

📌 Important Application Notes:

  • Zero Cost: The entire MEXT application process is completely FREE. Do not pay anyone for application assistance or "guaranteed placement"
  • Official Channels Only: Applications can ONLY be submitted through MAJA for Myanmar students
  • Keep Copies: Maintain copies of all submitted documents and correspondence
  • Email Check: Regularly check your email (including spam folder) after submission for updates and notifications
  • Contact Professors: For graduate students, contacting potential supervisors at Japanese universities before/during application significantly improves chances
04

Pro Tips for Success

🎯

Start Extremely Early - 1 Year Ahead

MEXT receives thousands of applications worldwide with acceptance rate around 3-5% for Myanmar. Begin preparation at least 12 months before deadline. Strong, thorough preparation is your competitive advantage.

📊

Perfect Your Research Plan (Graduate Applicants)

This is THE MOST CRITICAL document! Be highly specific about research methodology, objectives, and expected outcomes. Show deep understanding of current literature. Avoid vague, overly ambitious, or unrealistic plans. Get feedback from professors.

🎓

Contact Japanese Professors 6+ Months Early

Research potential supervisors at Japanese universities in your field. Email them with your research plan and CV. A professor's preliminary acceptance DRAMATICALLY strengthens your application and university placement chances.

📝

Ensure Perfect Documentation

All documents must be officially certified with university stamps, properly translated to English, and clearly organized. Even ONE missing or improperly certified document = automatic rejection. Double-check everything!

💪

Strong Academic Record Matters

While minimum GPA is 2.30/3.00, successful candidates typically have 2.80+ GPA or higher. If your GPA is lower, compensate with strong research plan, publications, work experience, or recommendation letters.

🗣️

Master Interview Skills

Practice explaining your study/research plan clearly and confidently in English. Prepare answers for: Why Japan? Why this field? What are your post-graduation plans? How will you contribute to Myanmar? Practice with friends or mentors.

🌸

Demonstrate Genuine Japan Interest

Show genuine interest in Japanese culture, language, society, and academic environment. Explain specifically why studying in Japan (not just any country) is important for your academic and career goals.

👥

Connect with MEXT Alumni Network

Contact MAJA members and current/former MEXT scholars from Myanmar. They provide invaluable firsthand insights about application process, interview questions, life in Japan, and university recommendations. Join MAJA Facebook group!
