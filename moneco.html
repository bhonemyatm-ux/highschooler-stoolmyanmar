About the University

Monywa University of Economics was established in Monywa Township, Sagaing Region in 1993. It was later upgraded (institute stage) and renamed as Monywa University of Economics in 2014. The university focuses on economics and business-related education for Upper Myanmar.

Why Choose MUE?

📊

Economics & Business Specialization

Offers degrees and diplomas in economics, commerce, statistics and business administration fields.

🎓

Multiple Degree Levels

MUE lists undergraduate degrees and graduate programs including MBA/MPA and higher degrees (program availability can vary by intake).

📍

Upper Myanmar Access

Located in Monywa, providing a pathway for students in Sagaing Region and nearby areas.
