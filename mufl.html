Academic Programs

Foreign language degree programmes for Upper Myanmar

🌐

BA Foreign Languages

Bachelor of Arts (BA)

Core 4-year degree offered through 11 language departments with intensive training in speaking, listening, reading and writing skills.

Duration: 4 Years Full-time
Min. Marks: High matriculation + language aptitude
Prerequisites: Strong English proficiency required
English German French Japanese
📖

Language Majors

11 Specializations

Students specialize in one primary foreign language from 11 departments while studying English as second language and Myanmar language.

Duration: 4 Years Major + Minor
Min. Marks: Regional department cut-offs
Prerequisites: Choose 1 major from 11 languages
Chinese Korean Spanish Russian Italian

Limited MA Programmes
