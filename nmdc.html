About NMDC

National Management Degree College (NMDC) is a management-focused college in Yangon. It was established as National Management College (NMC) in 2004 and later promoted as National Management Degree College (NMDC) in 2016.

NMDC lists bachelor programs in Business Management, English for Professional Purposes, Journalism, and Tourism & Hospitality Management, and it also lists BEF (Economics & Finance) in its admissions/program selections.

Why Choose NMDC?

🏙️

Central Yangon location

Based on Merchant Road in Botahtaung Township—easy access from many parts of Yangon.

📈

Career-focused degrees

Programs align with common career paths in business, media, tourism, and professional English.

📝

Online admission

NMDC provides an online admission form for multiple programs (BBM, BA(EPP), BA(Journalism), BTHM, BEF).
