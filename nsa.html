About the University

Naypyitaw State Academy (NSA) is a state-run university in Myanmar’s national capital Naypyidaw, located in Zabuthiri Township. It opened on 9 November 2022 to support higher education opportunities for students in Naypyidaw Union Territory, including children of civil servants.

Why Choose NSA?

🏛️

Multi-disciplinary Faculties

Includes faculties such as arts, science, law, economics, medicine, social studies and sports.

📍

Study in the Capital

Designed to help students from Naypyidaw study without relocating to Yangon or Mandalay.

📚

Growing Academic Programs

Official reporting states expansion to multiple faculties and specializations within a few academic years.

Recent Event: 2-Year Anniversary Ceremony

Myanmar’s Ministry of Information reported that NSA marked its 2-year anniversary with a ceremony at the academy’s multipurpose hall, and described the academy’s expansion from early intakes to multiple faculties and specializations.

What was reported

  • NSA was opened in 2022 and reached its second anniversary milestone.
  • Reporting noted growth in faculties/specializations and student intake over time.
  • Facilities mentioned include internet access, e-library, and ongoing infrastructure support.
