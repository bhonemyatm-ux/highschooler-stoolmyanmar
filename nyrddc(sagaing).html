About NYRDDC (Sagaing)

Nationalities Youth Resource Development Degree College (Sagaing) was established in 2000 alongside its sister institution in Yangon, under the Ministry of Border Affairs. Located on the Mandalay-Monywa Highway in Ohndaw Village, the college sits on a spacious 61.93-acre campus.

As of 2025, the college has produced 2,538 degree holders including 560 BA degrees, 981 BSc degrees, 139 BTech, and 858 AGTI diplomas. Additionally, the college offers graduate programs with 111 MA, 216 MSc, 32 PhD, 625 BE, 34 ME, and 207 BTech graduates. The institution focuses on developing skilled human resources from ethnic regions to serve in government administration and regional development.
