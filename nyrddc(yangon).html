About NYRDDC (Yangon)

Nationalities Youth Resource Development Degree College (Yangon) was established on June 15, 2000, under the Ministry of Border Affairs to develop human resources from border areas and national races. Initially opened at Tiger Philanthropic School in Hlaing Township, the college moved to its current 31.516-acre campus in Dagon Seikkan Township on July 5, 2005.

The college offers Bachelor of Arts, Bachelor of Science, AGTI Diploma (engineering), and BTech programs, focusing on preparing youth from ethnic regions for effective civil service and contributing to socio-economic development in border areas.
