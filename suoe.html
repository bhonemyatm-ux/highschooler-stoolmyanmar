About the University

Sagaing University of Education is described online as a teacher education institution offering Bachelor of Education and higher degrees. The “Sagaing Institute of Education” page lists programs including B.Ed, M.Ed, MPhil, PhD and Post Graduate Diploma in Multimedia Arts (PGDMA).

Why Choose SUOE?

Professional Trainings
