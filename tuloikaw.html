About the University

နည်းပညာတက္ကသိုလ် (လွိုင်ကော်) (Technological University, Loikaw – TU Loikaw) သည် ကယားပြည်နယ်၊ လွိုင်ကော်မြို့နယ်အတွင်း တည်ရှိသော အစိုးရပိုင် နည်းပညာတက္ကသိုလ်တစ်ခုဖြစ်သည်။ ၁၉၈၂ ခုနှစ်တွင် Government Technical High School အဖြစ် စတင်တည်ထောင်ခဲ့ပြီး၊ အဆင့်မြှင့်တင်မှုများအပြီး ၂၀၀၇ ခုနှစ် ဇန်နဝါရီ ၂၀ ရက်နေ့တွင် Technological University အဖြစ် တိုးမြှင့်တင်ခဲ့သည်။

Available Programs

Main engineering disciplines at TU (Loikaw)

🏗️

Civil Engineering

B.E.

Structures, construction, surveying, transportation, and infrastructure development.

Duration: Typically 6 years (varies by policy/intake)
Prerequisites: Matriculation exam (science)
Structural Analysis Surveying Concrete Technology Geotechnical Engineering
📡

Electronics & Communication Engineering (EC)

B.E.

Electronics, signals, networks, and communication systems.

Duration: Typically 6 years (varies by policy/intake)
Prerequisites: Matriculation exam (science)
Digital Electronics Communication Systems Signal Processing Networking Basics
⚡

Electrical Power Engineering (EP)

B.E.

Power systems, transmission, distribution, machines, and control.

Duration: Typically 6 years (varies by policy/intake)
Prerequisites: Matriculation exam (science)
Electrical Machines Power System Analysis Protection Control Systems
⚙️

Mechanical Engineering

B.E.

Design, manufacturing, thermal systems, and mechanical power.

Duration: Typically 6 years (varies by policy/intake)
Prerequisites: Matriculation exam (science)
Thermodynamics Fluid Mechanics Machine Design Manufacturing
