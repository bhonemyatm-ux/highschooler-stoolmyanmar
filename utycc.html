About the University

University of Technology (Yatanarpon Cyber City), abbreviated as UTYCC, is located at Yatanarpon Cyber City in Mandalay Region. Public sources describe UTYCC as being founded in 2010 and offering multiple engineering programs across ICT, electronics, materials and precision engineering.

Why Choose UTYCC?

💻

Strong ICT Focus

UTYCC offers engineering programs in Information Science & Technology and Computer Engineering.

📡

Engineering Variety

Programs include Electronics Engineering, Materials & Metallurgy, and Mechanical Precision & Automation.

🎓

Postgraduate Pathways

Public listings include M.E. programs and a Ph.D program in Information Technology.

📍

Cyber City Location

Located in Yatanarpon Cyber City near Pyin Oo Lwin, linking study with a technology-zone environment.
