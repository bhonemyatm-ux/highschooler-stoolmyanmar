About the University

Yezin Agricultural University (YAU) is a higher education institution specializing in agriculture in Myanmar, located in Yezin, Nay Pyi Taw. YAU traces its origins back to 1924 (Burma Agricultural College and Research Institute) and moved to the Yezin campus in 1973.

Agronomy Research (Crop Productivity & Climate-Smart Agriculture)

YAU’s Department of Agronomy reports research activities in crop production, varietal selection, crop physiology, crop nutrition, weed science, good agricultural practices, seed technology, postharvest technology, cultural practices, and climate-smart agriculture.
