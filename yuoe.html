About the University

Yangon University of Education is a professional institute of pedagogy offering teacher-training and education programs. Its official academic page lists B.Ed programs (including a five-year on-campus course), a modular M.Ed program, MA (Teaching of English as a Foreign Language), and PhD (Education).

Why Choose YUOE?

👩‍🏫

Teacher Training Focus

B.Ed and M.Ed programs are designed for pre-service and in-service teacher education pathways.

🗣️

TEFL Path

YUOE lists MA (Teaching of English as a Foreign Language) for those aiming for English teaching specialization.

🎓

Postgraduate Options

YUOE lists PhD (Education) and professional diploma programs (e.g., PGDMA, Dip in ELTM, Dip in ECCD).
