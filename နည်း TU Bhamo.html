About the University

Technological University (Bamaw/Banmaw/Bhamo) began as Technological College (Banmaw) in December 2001, with the main building opened on 11 February 2002 and another two-storey building opened on 10 July 2005. It was upgraded to Technological University on 20 January 2007.
