About the University

Technological University (Hinthada) started as a Technical High School (T.H.S) on 1 August 1977 in the south part of Tar Ngar Sge Quarter, Hinthada Township, Ayeyarwady Region. The institution was later upgraded (Government Technological College stage), and the college moved to the present university site on 21 April 2003.
