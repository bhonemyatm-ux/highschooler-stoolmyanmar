About TU-Myeik

TU (Myeik) offers 4 B.E. programmes—Civil, EC, EP, and Mechanical—with a 6-year duration. [web:421]

Why TU-Myeik?

🏝️

Regional relevance

Engineering skills are important for Tanintharyi’s transport, coastal infrastructure, and regional development needs.

Upgraded to TU
